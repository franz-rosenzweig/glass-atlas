<h1 align="center">Glass Atlas docs</h1>
<p align="center">An interactive 3D atlas of real human anatomy, built around one teaching case.</p>

These pages explain Glass Atlas the way I'd show it to you sitting next to me: what's on the screen, what each instrument lets you do, and where the numbers come from. If you're new, read "What is Glass Atlas?" first, and then take the tour in the app itself, which is the quickest way to see how the pieces fit together. The pages under "Under the hood" are the working notes for running and changing the atlas, and you only need them if you're working on the code.

It's all general education, not medical advice. The patient is a teaching case, and in real care a patient's own clinicians decide.
